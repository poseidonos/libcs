## Review Requirements

- **Minor changes** (bug fixes, docs, tests): require +1 from one maintainer
- **API changes** (new functions, changed signatures): require +1 from two maintainers
- **Breaking changes** (removed/renamed APIs): require maintainer consensus + migration guide

## Becoming a Maintainer

Maintainers are added by consensus of the existing maintainers. Contributors
who consistently provide high-quality PRs and reviews may be invited to join.
